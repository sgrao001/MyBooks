From Teens to Senior Teens — Such a Long Journey

Fifty years ago, we walked out of our school gates— fresh, radiant, with dreams and excitement For the future ahead

We said goodbye to classmates, filled autograph books with thoughtful notes and signatures, promising to stay in touch forever.

Some were for friends, Some for secret crushes. We never said it then— now we smile.

Uniforms were folded away, and we stepped on to Life's great highway

The years rolled on.

Life had other plans Dreams became deadlines. Pocket money became EMIs. Report cards became report meetings. And before we knew it, the mirrors showed silver, But the smiles... were still sixteen.

Life took each of us different journeys— yet somehow, it brought us all back here.

Along the way, we lost dear friends— now forever young in our hearts.

And then, almost before we knew it… fifty years had passed. We met again. We were sixteen then. Today, we are sixty-five. One smile. One handshake. One embrace… and the years vanished.

Laughter returned. Memories rushed back Those crushes of our teens Still looking youthful and radiant.

Over coffee— or a sip of amber and brown— we were sixteen again.

So let us toast, dear friends—

to friendships that outlast time, to memories grown richer, to lives well lived, and to the years ahead.

We were once Teens. Today, we are Senior Teens.

May our laughter stay young, our bonds, evergreen, and may time disappear when we meet again.

For friendships, like the best school lessons, never grow old.

Mother Cauvery

You flow gently and unhurried, With ageless grace and beauty, Serene and calm. Oh, Mother Cauvery, the daughter of the Mountains You have witnessed the ages of history. Within your gentle bosom Lie countless moments of time gone by.

You have watched The passage of generations, The rise and fall of kingdoms, The joys and sorrows of humanity.

You give solace to one and all, To the sons and daughters, To the husbands and wives, Grieving souls who have Bid farewell to those they loved. Blessed are those who rest in your embrace

You bring smiles To the tillers of the land. Giving life to the fields You comfort every heart you touch

When you are angry, Cauvery, You surge and gush in a torrent. You flood and you roar, But your anger is fleeting. Even in your fury,you leave behind the gift of life. And when peace returns, You inspire songs and stories, Paeans of prose and poetry, Filling hearts with hope, Happiness, And the promise of tomorrow

The keeper of memories

Old Father Time, memories, The Keeper of the silent songs of the heart, Who stirs a restless soul, With memories stored in The fog of the mind.

Old Father Time, Who plays the strings of the heart, And stirs those whispers, Of a bruised heart.

Old Father Time, Who preserves the fragments of The forgotten yesterdays, Some pleasant, Some weary. Like the waves of the sea, Washing the sandy beach, Golden and peaceful, With the eternal rhythm of the sea.

When He feels it is time to close, The pages of Time, He opens yet another page, Reminding us Of new chapters yet to come.

A thousand miles, Someone, Somewhere

My heart danced with joy When I heard that voice again. I am not a poet, Yet a million songs arose within my heart.

A million melodies, Awakened by the magic of forgotten time. A million magical moments, A million sonatas, A million dreams,

Swirling like illusions through time, Like a mirage in the desert of memory, A vision that beckons The weary traveller Across the endless sands of time.

My heart, Broken into a million pieces A million years ago, Danced once more With the joy of rekindled passion.

Those million songs Became a healing balm

Autumn Sonata

A gentle rain on an autumn morning, Like a sonata greeting the dawn, Like a waltz that softly accompanies The melody of a waking day. Yet my heart lay dry and empty, And then I saw her coming

Like a morning sonata My heart missed a beat And it missed many more beats My mind a whirlwind of emotions As she passed me like a fragrant breeze

Years have passed since that thunderclap She moved on and I moved on Yet within my heart Lives that radiant smile. Within my heart Echoes that first thunderclap, That first awakening of love.

Days passed into months and decades Those memories woven Into beautiful brocades of time. And then one day she waltzed back into my life Like an Autumn Sonata in my weary life My heart once again Resonated with forgotten music. The embers of lost emotions Glowed once more, And love returned Like a thunderclap, Once again.
